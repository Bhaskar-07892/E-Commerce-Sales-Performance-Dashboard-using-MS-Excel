
# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales & Performance Dashboard | Excel

An interactive **E-Commerce Sales & Performance Dashboard** built using Microsoft Excel to analyze sales, profitability, customer behavior, product categories, and delivery performance.

This project transforms raw e-commerce transaction data into meaningful business insights using PivotTables, PivotCharts, KPI cards, and interactive slicers.


## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profitability.
- Identify top-performing regions and product categories.
- Compare yearly revenue performance.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Analyze revenue by payment method.
- Monitor returned orders and return rates.
- Evaluate delivery time and shipping costs.
- Build an interactive business intelligence dashboard.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

The following observations are based on the current dashboard and PivotTable summaries.

| Analysis | Observation |
|---|---|
| Yearly Revenue | 2024 generated approximately 2.93M, the highest among the displayed years. |
| Regional Performance | South generated approximately 1.30M in revenue. |
| Payment Methods | Credit Card contributed approximately 2.06M in revenue. |
| Product Categories | Electronics generated approximately 3.32M in revenue. |
| Customer Analysis | Female customers contributed approximately 2.85M in revenue. |
| Delivery Performance | Overall average delivery time was 5 days. |
| Returns | Returned rate was approximately 6%. |
| Profitability | Grocery recorded a negative aggregate profit contribution of approximately 9.2K. |

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Investigate costs, discounts, and pricing in the Grocery category.
- Review delivery delays in regions with higher average delivery times.
- Monitor Electronics inventory and profitability.
- Analyze return patterns by product category and region.
- Compare payment methods based on revenue and transaction costs.
- Investigate yearly revenue changes before making business decisions.

*These observations indicate areas for further analysis; they do not establish the causes of the results.*
